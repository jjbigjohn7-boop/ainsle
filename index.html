Questions

Frequently Asked

When can I review my Medicare plan?
During Medicare's Annual Enrollment Period, October 15 through December 7, you can review and change your coverage for the coming year. Call us and a licensed agent will go over your options.
Can the assistant who answers the phone give me a quote?
No — and that's by design. Our assistant takes your information and arranges your callback. All quotes and recommendations come from a licensed agent.
What is KonnectMD?
A monthly virtual healthcare membership: 24/7 access to licensed providers by phone or video, no visit fees, and discounts on medications. It is not health insurance and works alongside your coverage or on its own.
How do I book a review?
Call (813) 791-8131, call our 24/7 line at (888) 702-5321, or use the Book an Appointment button above — whatever is easiest for you.
